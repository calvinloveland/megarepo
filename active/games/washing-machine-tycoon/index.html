🧺 Welcome to Washing Machine Tycoon

Your goal: Build the world's most trusted washing machine brand.

Here's how to get started:

1 Go to Design Studio and create your first washing machine model. Choose components that balance cost, durability, and customer appeal.
2 Go to Factory, assign your model to the production line, configure speed and quality control, then start production.
3 Machines are sold automatically. Watch the Dashboard for your cash, reputation, and event log.
4 When machines fail, handle warranty claims in the Service Department. Your response affects reputation and customer satisfaction.
5 Reinvest in R&D to unlock new components. Adapt to regulations and outpace competitors.

Every machine you sell becomes a long-term responsibility. Choose your components wisely. 💪
