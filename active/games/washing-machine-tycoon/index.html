🧺 How to Play Washing Machine Tycoon

Your goal: Build the world's most trusted washing machine brand.

🎯 Core Loop
1 Design Studio — Choose every component (drum, motor, bearings, etc.) to create a machine model.
2 Factory — Assign your model to a production line. Balance speed vs quality control.
3 Dashboard — Watch sales, cash, and reputation grow. Handle warranty claims as they arrive.
4 Service — When machines fail, decide how to respond. Your choices affect your brand.
💡 Key Tips
  • Premium bearings are the single best investment — cheap bearings cause catastrophic failures
  • Quality control slows production but reduces defects. A reputation takes years to build and days to lose
  • Watch the regulations — new standards punish old designs. A 1970 machine becomes illegal by 1990
  • Invest in R&D — tech unlocks require research spending. Without it, you will be stuck with 1940s components
  • Hire enough technicians for your machine base. Understaffed service = angry customers
  • Use the Clone button on existing models as a starting point for new designs
  • The game starts in 1945 and runs through the present day — each decade brings new tech, regulations, and competitors
📊 Key Metrics
Reputation — Brand trust (0-100). Affects sales and customer patience.
Customer Satisfaction — Average across all active machines.
Market Share — Your portion of the total market. AI competitors want it too.
Pending Claims — Open warranty cases needing resolution.
