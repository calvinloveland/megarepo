EPSON TM-T88V
THERMAL RECEIPT PRINTER
=======================

CONNECTION
USB. Appears as one raw
character device:
  /dev/usb/lp0
Write bytes to it and paper
comes out. No driver, no
print queue, no spooler.

LANGUAGE
ESC/POS. Plain text plus
escape codes for style,
alignment, images, cutting.

PAPER
203 dpi thermal.
58mm roll = 384 dots wide
80mm roll = 576 dots wide
58mm gives 32 characters.
Anything wider is clipped
on the right edge.

CONTROL CODES
ESC @        reset printer
ESC a 1      centre text
ESC E 1/0    bold on/off
GS ! 1/0     double height
GS V 66 0    feed and cut

IMAGES
Any picture can be printed
as a 1-bit bitmap. Photos
want dithering. QR codes
must NOT be dithered or
they stop scanning.
